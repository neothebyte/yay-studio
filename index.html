What it does

  • Writes posts in Burmese, English and other languages from a short topic.
  • Suggests ranked topic ideas and turns news articles into posts.
  • Plans posts on a content calendar.
  • Optionally saves your posts and images to your own Google Drive.

Yay Studio is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It is not a public service and has no user accounts.
