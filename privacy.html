Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Yay Studio is a personal content tool that runs locally on its owner's computer. This policy explains what happens when you connect Google Drive.

Google Drive access

  • Yay Studio asks only for the drive.file permission. It can create files in your Drive and see only the files it created. It cannot read, change or delete any other file in your Drive.
  • It uses that permission solely to save the posts and images you choose to export, into a folder named “Yay Studio Exports”.
  • It also reads your Google account email address, only to show which account is connected.

Where your data is stored

Sign-in tokens, posts and settings are stored only on the computer running Yay Studio. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any server other than Google's APIs and the AI provider you configure for writing posts. Google user data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or to train AI models.

Removing access

Use “Disconnect” in Yay Studio's settings, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already saved to your Drive stay yours.

Yay Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
